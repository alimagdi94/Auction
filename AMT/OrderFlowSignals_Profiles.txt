  PROFILE 1 — HFT MODE

  Timeframe: M1 or M2
  Session: London open (08:00–10:00 GMT) or NY open (13:30–15:30 GMT)
  Goal: Catch short-duration momentum bursts with fast re-entry capability

NOTES — HFT MODE:
  • Use M1 chart. M2 is acceptable. Do NOT use on M5+ — signal logic is tuned for speed.
  • Run only during London (08:00–10:00 GMT) or NY open (13:30–15:30 GMT).
    Outside these windows set InpShowSignals=false or widen InpOffSessionMult.
  • Risk per trade: 0.25–0.5% max. HFT signals are frequent, some will fail.
  • Expected signal frequency: 3–8 per session hour in active markets.
  • Delta divergence signals (c2) are the highest-conviction setups in this profile.
  • Always confirm with DOM/level 2 if available — this profile is delta-forward.
  • Spread sensitivity: InpMaxSpreadPts is critical. Widen spread = bad fill = bad P&L.
  • Backtesting note: SIG_FREQ_BARS=1 will show many historical signals.
    In live trading, fill latency will naturally reduce effective trade frequency.

NOTES — SCALPING MODE:
  • Use M5 as primary. M3 works for faster scalpers but increases noise.
  • The BOTH frequency gate (bars + time) is intentional — scalping profits from
    quality, not quantity. Expect 2–5 signals per session.
  • Risk per trade: 0.5–1.0%. Higher conviction justifies larger size vs HFT.
  • Entry: enter at market on next tick after signal fires (bar close).
    Do NOT wait for a pullback — scalping signals are momentum-based.
  • Target: 1.5–2× the bar range at signal time as TP.
    Stop: bar low (buy) or bar high (sell) + 1× ATR buffer.
  • Instrument-specific InpMaxSpreadPts guide:
      EURUSD  → 10     GBPUSD → 15     USDJPY → 10
      XAUUSD  → 60     USOIL  → 40     NAS100 → 30
  • InpMinVolPctile=25 means roughly 1 in 4 bars qualify by volume alone.
    Combined with threshold=65 and session filter, expect very clean setups.
  • Divergence signals (is_delta_divergence=true bars) are the BEST scalp setups
    in this profile — they represent trapped traders and sharp reversals.
  • Do not scalp XAUUSD during NFP, FOMC, or CPI — set InpShowSignals=false.
