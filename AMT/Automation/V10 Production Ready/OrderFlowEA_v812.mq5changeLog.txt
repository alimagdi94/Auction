Pass 1 — SLTP-LOG-CLAMP (Medium)
trade_Send() silently clamps SL/TP to the broker's minimum distance but the success-path log in PlaceOrders() always used the original pre-clamp variables — so the journal said SL=1.08500 while the live order sat at 1.08480. Three coordinated changes: trade_Send() gains two double &outSL, double &outTP output parameters initialised to the incoming values before the clamp block; each clamp branch now assigns to them and fires a LogWarning() with the before/after values; PlaceOrders() declares sentSL/sentTP, passes them through, and logs them in both the success log and DrawTradeEntry(). The analysis-mode log correctly keeps sl/tp since that path never calls trade_Send().
Pass 2 — EQUITY-INPUT-VALIDATE (Low)
InpMaxEquityProfit and InpMaxEquityLoss were the only monetary inputs with no OnInit() range check. Negative values were silently treated as disabled by the > 0.0 guard at the call site, masking what is almost always a data-entry mistake (e.g. -3000 instead of 3000). Two validation lines added after the InpHaltConsecLosses ordering check, returning INIT_PARAMETERS_INCORRECT with a descriptive Alert().
Pass 3 — RETCODE-SLEEP (Low)
Sleep(200) in trade_Send()'s requote retry loop blocked the entire MQL5 tick thread for up to 400 ms across two retries — exactly during the fast-market/news conditions that cause requotes, preventing ManagePositions() from updating trailing stops on open positions. Replaced with a GetTickCount64() spin-wait capped at 50 ms, which is sufficient for a broker price refresh. The separate Sleep(200) in ManagePositions()' own position-modification retry loop is a different path and out of scope for this patch.
